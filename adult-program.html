Adult Program

Our adult program offers traditional Jow Ga Kung Fu training in a supportive environment. Classes are designed to accommodate busy schedules while building strength, discipline, and community.

Strengthen Your Mind and Body

We understand the challenges of balancing demanding work schedules, travel commitments, and other responsibilities. We offer Jow Ga classes tailored for adults of all ages (18+) and a flexible class schedule allows you to train at your own pace, whether you're looking for short-term or long-term training.

Drop in anytime for a free trial class. Simply fill out our quick sign-up form below to let us know when you plan on visiting!
