Support Our Mission to Empower Youth

Your generous donation helps us provide free martial arts programs and leadership training to youth in Washington, DC. For over 30 years, we've been building character, discipline, and confidence in young people through the practice of Kung Fu.

Every contribution directly supports our mission to keep the DC youth healthy, in school and on the path to success.

Your donation is 100% tax-deductible. After your payment, Zeffy will email you a receipt that you can keep for your tax records.

We use Zeffy to ensure 100% of your purchase goes directly to our mission. Zeffy charges us $0 in fees, but they will ask for an optional tip at checkout to keep their platform running for nonprofits. You are welcome to change this tip amount to 'Other' and enter $0 if you prefer.

How Your Donations Make a Difference

Your contributions fund special events, workshops, and programs that create life-changing experiences for children in our community.

Summer Camp Programs

Your donations provide equipment, uniforms, and instruction for our free summer camp programs. These intensive sessions keep DC kids engaged, active, and learning throughout the summer months when they need it most.

Camp Open Arms Workshop

Thanks to donor support, we host specialized workshops like Camp Open Arms—a two-day program designed for children with disabilities. These inclusive events provide adaptive martial arts training in a supportive, encouraging environment where every child can thrive.
